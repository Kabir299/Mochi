Mochi Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Mochi: Habit Tracker & Planner ("the app") is made by [your name or company]. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your phone

Your habits, check-ins, notes, moods, focus time, water and food logs, setup answers (goals, body goal, weight if given, wake-up time, working hours) and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have a copy. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, removes them.

What is sent when you use AI features

AI features (setup plan, analysis, coach, plan my day, brain dump, food estimates) only run when you tap them. The app then sends a summary to our AI server: habit names, completion percentages, streaks, the text you typed, your setup answers and, if you log them, daily water, calorie and protein totals. It does not send your name, contacts, location or device identifiers.

Our server passes that summary to an AI provider (currently Groq, with Google Gemini as a backup) to produce the answer, and then forgets it. We don't store these requests or use them for advertising. The providers process them under their own API terms, which do not allow training on API data by default. To stop abuse, the server briefly keeps a count of requests per IP address in memory; it is not written to disk.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Things the app does not do

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone except the AI provider, as above
  • Not intended for children under 13
