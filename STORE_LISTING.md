## Description

Tell Mochi what you want to build, your body goal and the hours you focus best. In a minute you
get a daily plan that fits your life, not a blank checklist.

**Plan your day, not just your habits**
• A timeline of your day with start times and durations
• Focus blocks placed in your most productive hours
• Smart reminders a few minutes before each task

**Stay motivated**
• A pet that cheers when you show up, grows with your streak and unlocks new looks
• Day streaks with monthly freezes, so one bad day doesn't undo a month
• Confetti, badges and a yearly heatmap

**Understand your progress**
• Daily score weighted by what matters most
• Charts, trends and insights
• Share a card of your week with friends

**Pro**
• AI coach: weekly analysis, chat and "plan my day"
• Brain dump: ramble and get a clean to-do list
• Water and food tracking with automatic calorie and protein estimates

Your data stays on your phone. No account needed.
